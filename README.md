# Hi, I'm Quiet115 👋

I build small, quiet Android apps and tools. No accounts, no ads, no telemetry: they do one job and keep your data on your phone.

*Hola 👋 Hago apps y herramientas pequeñas y discretas para Android, sin cuentas, sin anuncios y sin telemetría.*

## What I've built

| Project | What it does | Get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[QuietRingtones](https://github.com/Quiet115/QuietRingtones)** | Change your ringtone, notification and alarm sounds in one tap. Profiles, trimming, your own audio, custom themes. | [Latest release](https://github.com/Quiet115/QuietRingtones/releases/latest) · MIT |
| **[QuietDLP](https://github.com/Quiet115/QuietDLP)** | Open-source video, audio and photo downloader for Android, built on yt-dlp and gallery-dl. Tap *Share* and it downloads in the background. | [Latest release](https://github.com/Quiet115/QuietDLP/releases/latest) · GPL-3.0 |
| **[QuietCleanUp](https://github.com/Quiet115/QuietCleanUp-releases)** | Organizes your screenshots: reads the text on-device, detects which app each one came from, and lets you delete in groups, always with your approval. (Spanish) | [Latest release](https://github.com/Quiet115/QuietCleanUp-releases/releases/latest) |
| **[find-skills-safer](https://github.com/Quiet115/find-skills-safer)** | A Claude Code skill that finds other skills with a shortlist, quarantine review and explicit install gates. | MIT |
| **[skill-auditor](https://github.com/Quiet115/skill-auditor)** | Audits Claude skills and plugins before you install them: security, real benefit, token cost. | MIT |

The apps share one design system, so they feel like a family.

## Work with me

I can build a custom Android app or a focused tool for you, from the idea to a release people can install. If you have something in mind, open an issue or a discussion on any repository above and tell me what you need.
